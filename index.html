About

Compliance built like an engineer thinks

I lead anti-money-laundering and KYC operations for a virtual-asset business preparing for regulatory licensing — working at the intersection of financial-crime prevention, blockchain forensics and the risk-based approach. As AML Team Lead at Asterium I own the program end-to-end: KYC/EDD workflows, AML screening & risk scoring, transaction monitoring, sanctions, and the SOPs and controls that make it audit-ready.

As a Chainalysis Certified Advanced Investigator I trace on-chain activity across UTXO- and account-based assets, investigate mixing and obfuscation, and turn findings into defensible risk decisions. Earlier, as an Auditor at Binance, I tested the design and operating effectiveness of compliance controls and reviewed flagged accounts and SARs. I also build small, explainable RegTech tools — because the best controls are the ones you can explain to a regulator.
